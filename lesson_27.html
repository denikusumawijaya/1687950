Section 1: Spiral Review Warm-up (00:00 - 00:10)

Bagian 1: Pemanasan Tinjauan Spiral

Before we start, let's sharpen our algebra skills. Think of this like collecting items in a game.
Sebelum mulai, mari asah kemampuan aljabar kita. Anggap saja ini seperti mengumpulkan item dalam game.

Theory: Variables and Constants

A variable is a letter that represents an unknown number (like a player's level). A constant is a fixed number that does not change.
Variabel adalah huruf yang mewakili angka yang tidak diketahui (seperti level pemain). Konstanta adalah angka tetap yang tidak berubah.

Question 1: In the expression \(4x + 7\), which one is the variable?
Pada ekspresi \(4x + 7\), mana yang merupakan variabel?

Question 2: In the expression \(15 - y\), what is the constant?
Pada ekspresi \(15 - y\), apa yang merupakan konstanta?

Question 3: You have \(x\) gems and get 10 bonus. What is the expression for the total?
Kamu punya \(x\) permata dan dapat bonus 10. Berapakah ekspresinya?

Theory: Coefficients

A coefficient is a number multiplied by a variable. If you have \(3g\), the number 3 is the coefficient. It means 3 times the gold (\(g\)).
Koefisien adalah angka yang dikalikan dengan variabel. Jika kamu punya \(3g\), angka 3 adalah koefisien. Artinya 3 kali lipat emas (\(g\)).

Question 4: What is the coefficient of \(y\) in \(12 - 5y\)?
Berapakah koefisien dari \(y\) pada \(12 - 5y\)?

Question 5: Write an expression for "Three times the sum of gold (\(g\)) minus 5".
Tulis ekspresi untuk "Tiga kali jumlah emas (\(g\)) dikurangi 5".

Question 6: Simplify: \(3(g + 4) - 2\).
Sederhanakan: \(3(g + 4) - 2\).

Theory: Algebra in Geometry

In geometry, angles can also be represented by variables. If an angle is \(x\), double that angle is \(2x\).
Dalam geometri, sudut juga bisa diwakili oleh variabel. Jika sebuah sudut adalah \(x\), dua kali sudut itu adalah \(2x\).

Question 7: If an angle is \(x\), what is twice that angle?
Jika sebuah sudut adalah \(x\), berapakah dua kali sudut tersebut?

Question 8: If an angle is \(2x\), what is half of that angle?
Jika sebuah sudut adalah \(2x\), berapakah setengah dari sudut tersebut?

Question 9: Two angles add up to 180 degrees. If one is \(x\), what is the other?
Dua sudut dijumlahkan menjadi 180 derajat. Jika satuannya \(x\), berapakah sudut lainnya?

Question 10: If \(x = 50\), what is \(2x + 10\)?
Jika \(x = 50\), berapakah \(2x + 10\)?

Section 2: Introduction & Real-World Connection (00:10 - 00:15)

Bagian 2: Pengantar & Koneksi Dunia Nyata

Theory: Circles in Games

Have you ever looked at a mini-map or a radar in your favorite game? Developers use circle angles to determine the character's field of view.
Pernahkah kamu melihat mini-map atau radar di game favoritmu? Developer game menggunakan sudut lingkaran untuk menentukan sudut pandang karakter.

Question 1: Where do we often see circles in games?
Di mana kita sering melihat lingkaran di dalam game?

Question 2: What is the middle point of a radar called?
Apa nama titik tengah dari sebuah radar?

Question 3: Why do developers use circle angles?
Mengapa developer menggunakan sudut lingkaran?

Theory: Central vs Inscribed Angle

If you draw two lines from the center, you make a Central Angle. If you draw two lines from the edge, you make an Inscribed Angle.
Jika kamu menggambar dua garis dari pusat, kamu membuat Sudut Pusat. Jika dari tepi, kamu membuat Sudut Keliling.

Question 4: An angle with its vertex (point) at the center of the circle is called...
Sudut dengan titik puncaknya di pusat lingkaran disebut...

Question 5: An angle with its vertex on the edge of the circle is called...
Sudut dengan titik puncaknya di tepi lingkaran disebut...

Question 6: Look at the radar. The red lines meet at the center. What kind of angle is this?
Lihat radar. Garis merah bertemu di pusat. Sudut apakah ini?

Theory: The Arc (Busur)

An arc is a part of the circle's edge. Both Central and Inscribed angles "look" at an arc.
Busur adalah bagian dari tepi lingkaran. Baik sudut pusat maupun sudut keliling sama-sama "melihat" sebuah busur.

Question 7: What do we call the curved line on the edge of a circle?
Apa yang kita sebut untuk garis lengkung di tepi lingkaran?

Question 8: If a Central Angle and an Inscribed Angle look at the SAME arc, are they related?
Jika Sudut Pusat dan Sudut Keliling melihat busur yang SAMA, apakah mereka berhubungan?

Question 9: In a game, if the radar scans a certain arc, the central angle sees it from the...
Dalam game, jika radar memindai busur tertentu, sudut pusat melihatnya dari...

Question 10: The inscribed angle sees the arc from the...
Sudut keliling melihat busur dari...

Section 3: Review of Prior Knowledge (00:15 - 00:20)

Bagian 3: Tinjauan Pengetahuan Sebelumnya

Theory: Radius and Diameter

Radius (r) is the distance from the center to the edge. Diameter (d) is the distance from edge to edge, passing through the center. \( d = 2 \times r \)
Jari-jari (r) adalah jarak dari pusat ke tepi. Diameter (d) adalah jarak dari tepi ke tepi melalui pusat. \( d = 2 \times r \)

Question 1: What is the line from the center to the edge called?
Apa nama garis dari pusat ke tepi?

Question 2: What is the line from edge to edge through the center called?
Apa nama garis dari tepi ke tepi melalui pusat?

Question 3: If the radius \( r = 5 \), what is the diameter \( d \)?
Jika jari-jari \( r = 5 \), berapakah diameter \( d \)?

Theory: The Center Point

The center is the "boss" of the circle. All points on the edge are the same distance from the center.
Pusat adalah "bos" dari lingkaran. Semua titik di tepi memiliki jarak yang sama dari pusat.

Question 4: What is the middle point of a circle called?
Apa nama titik tengah lingkaran?

Question 5: If point A is 10 cm from the center, and point B is on the edge, how far is B from the center?
Jika titik A berjarak 10 cm dari pusat, dan titik B ada di tepi, berapakah jarak B dari pusat?

Question 6: If diameter is 20, what is the radius?
Jika diameter adalah 20, berapakah jari-jarinya?

Theory: Angles

An angle is formed when two lines meet at a point. We measure angles in degrees (\(^\circ\)). A full circle is \(360^\circ\), and a half circle is \(180^\circ\).
Sudut terbentuk saat dua garis bertemu di satu titik. Kita mengukur sudut dalam derajat (\(^\circ\)). Satu lingkaran penuh adalah \(360^\circ\), dan setengah lingkaran adalah \(180^\circ\).

Question 7: What unit do we use to measure angles?
Satuan apa yang digunakan untuk mengukur sudut?

Question 8: How many degrees is a full circle?
Berapa derajat satu lingkaran penuh?

Question 9: How many degrees is a half circle (straight line)?
Berapa derajat setengah lingkaran (garis lurus)?

Question 10: If an angle is \(90^\circ\), it is called a...
Jika sebuah sudut adalah \(90^\circ\), itu disebut sudut...

Section 4: Problem Introduction (00:20 - 00:30)

Bagian 4: Pengantar Masalah

Theory: The 2:1 Rule (The Golden Rule)

If a Central Angle and an Inscribed Angle look at the SAME arc, the Central Angle is always twice (2x) the Inscribed Angle.
Jika Sudut Pusat dan Sudut Keliling menghadap busur yang SAMA, Sudut Pusat selalu dua kali (2x) Sudut Keliling.

\(\text{Central Angle} = 2 \times \text{Inscribed Angle}\)

\(\text{Sudut Pusat} = 2 \times \text{Sudut Keliling}\)

Question 1: If the inscribed angle is \(50^\circ\), what is the central angle?
Jika sudut keliling adalah \(50^\circ\), berapakah sudut pusatnya?

Question 2: If the central angle is \(100^\circ\), what is the inscribed angle?
Jika sudut pusat adalah \(100^\circ\), berapakah sudut kelilingnya?

Question 3: Which angle is bigger if they look at the same arc?
Sudut mana yang lebih besar jika menghadap busur yang sama?

Theory: Why 2:1? (For Critical Thinkers)

Imagine the arc is a pizza crust. The center angle "sees" the whole crust from the center. The edge angle "sees" it from the side. Because the center is the "boss", it sees it twice as wide!
Bayangkan busur adalah pinggiran pizza. Sudut pusat "melihat" seluruh pinggiran dari pusat. Sudut tepi "melihat"nya dari pinggir. Karena pusat adalah "bos", dia melihatnya dua kali lebih lebar!

Question 4: If central angle \(= 2 \times\) inscribed angle, then inscribed angle \(=\) ? central angle.
Jika sudut pusat \(= 2 \times\) sudut keliling, maka sudut keliling \(=\) ? sudut pusat.

Question 5: If the central angle is \(80^\circ\), and an inscribed angle looks at the same arc. What is the inscribed angle?
Jika sudut pusat \(80^\circ\), dan sudut keliling menghadap busur yang sama. Berapakah sudut kelilingnya?

Question 6: If the central angle is \(x\), the inscribed angle is...
Jika sudut pusat adalah \(x\), sudut keliling adalah...

Theory: Cyclic Quadrilateral (Segi Empat Tali Busur)

If a 4-sided shape is inside a circle, the opposite angles add up to \(180^\circ\). Like two puzzle pieces making a straight line.
Jika bangun 4-sisi ada di dalam lingkaran, sudut yang berhadapan dijumlahkan menjadi \(180^\circ\). Seperti dua keping puzzle membentuk garis lurus.

\(\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ\)

\(\angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ\)

Question 7: In a cyclic quadrilateral, if \(\angle A = 80^\circ\), what is \(\angle C\)?
Pada segi empat tali busur, jika \(\angle A = 80^\circ\), berapakah \(\angle C\)?

Question 8: If \(\angle B = 100^\circ\), what is \(\angle D\)?
Jika \(\angle B = 100^\circ\), berapakah \(\angle D\)?

Question 9: What do opposite angles in a cyclic quadrilateral add up to?
Berapa jumlah sudut yang berhadapan dalam segi empat tali busur?

Question 10: If \(\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ\), and \(\angle A = x\), what is \(\angle C\)?
Jika \(\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ\), dan \(\angle A = x\), berapakah \(\angle C\)?

Section 5: Guided Practice (00:30 - 00:45)

Bagian 5: Latihan Terbimbing

Theory: Calculating Inscribed Angles

If you know the Central Angle, just divide it by 2 to find the Inscribed Angle. Let's look at the mini-map. The center is \(O\). The edge point is \(C\).
Jika kamu tahu Sudut Pusat, bagi dengan 2 untuk mencari Sudut Keliling. Mari lihat mini-map. Pusatnya \(O\). Titik tepinya \(C\).

Question 1: If the central angle is \(120^\circ\), what is the inscribed angle?
Jika sudut pusat \(120^\circ\), berapakah sudut kelilingnya?

Question 2: If the inscribed angle is \(30^\circ\), what is the central angle?
Jika sudut keliling \(30^\circ\), berapakah sudut pusatnya?

Question 3: If central angle is \(90^\circ\), what is the inscribed angle facing the same arc?
Jika sudut pusat \(90^\circ\), berapakah sudut keliling yang menghadap busur sama?

Theory: Inscribed Angle Facing Diameter (Thales' Theorem)

If an inscribed angle looks at the diameter (half the circle), it is ALWAYS \(90^\circ\) (a right angle). Like a perfect corner!
Jika sudut keliling menghadap diameter (setengah lingkaran), itu SELALU \(90^\circ\) (sudut siku-siku). Seperti sudut yang sempurna!

\(\text{Inscribed Angle facing Diameter} = 90^\circ\)

Question 4: If an inscribed angle faces the diameter, what is its size?
Jika sudut keliling menghadap diameter, berapakah besarnya?

Question 5: If a triangle is inside a circle and its base is the diameter, the angle opposite the base is...
Jika segitiga ada di dalam lingkaran dan alasnya diameter, sudut di hadapan alasnya adalah...

Question 6: If the central angle is \(180^\circ\) (diameter), the inscribed angle facing it is...
Jika sudut pusat \(180^\circ\) (diameter), sudut keliling yang menghadapnya adalah...

Theory: Finding Missing Angles in Cyclic Quadrilaterals

Remember the puzzle? Opposite angles add to \(180^\circ\). If you know one, subtract from 180 to find the other.
Ingat puzzle-nya? Sudut berhadapan dijumlah jadi \(180^\circ\). Jika tahu satu, kurangi dari 180 untuk mencari yang lain.

Question 7: In a cyclic quadrilateral, \(\angle A = 70^\circ\). What is \(\angle C\)?
Pada segi empat tali busur, \(\angle A = 70^\circ\). Berapakah \(\angle C\)?

Question 8: If \(\angle B = 110^\circ\), what is \(\angle D\)?
Jika \(\angle B = 110^\circ\), berapakah \(\angle D\)?

Question 9: If \(\angle A = x\) and \(\angle C = 2x\), what is \(x\)? (Hint: \(x + 2x = 180\))
Jika \(\angle A = x\) dan \(\angle C = 2x\), berapakah \(x\)? (Petunjuk: \(x + 2x = 180\))

Question 10: If \(\angle A = 3x\) and \(\angle C = x\), what is \(\angle C\)?
Jika \(\angle A = 3x\) dan \(\angle C = x\), berapakah \(\angle C\)?

Theory: Algebra in Circles

Sometimes angles use variables like \(x\). If Central Angle \(= 4x\) and Inscribed Angle \(= 60^\circ\), we know \(4x = 2 \times 60^\circ\).
Terkadang sudut menggunakan variabel seperti \(x\). Jika Sudut Pusat \(= 4x\) dan Sudut Keliling \(= 60^\circ\), kita tahu \(4x = 2 \times 60^\circ\).

Question 1: If central angle is \(4x\) and inscribed angle is \(40^\circ\). What is \(x\)?
Jika sudut pusat \(4x\) dan sudut keliling \(40^\circ\). Berapakah \(x\)?

Question 2: If central angle is \(2x\) and inscribed angle is \(x\), and the inscribed angle is \(50^\circ\). What is the central angle?
Jika sudut pusat \(2x\) dan sudut keliling \(x\), dan sudut kelilingnya \(50^\circ\). Berapakah sudut pusatnya?

Question 3: If central angle is \((x + 20)^\circ\) and inscribed angle is \(50^\circ\). Find \(x\).
Jika sudut pusat \((x + 20)^\circ\) dan sudut keliling \(50^\circ\). Carilah \(x\).

Theory: Same Arc, Same Angle

If two inscribed angles look at the exact same arc, they are twins! They have the same size.
Jika dua sudut keliling melihat busur yang sama persis, mereka adalah kembar! Mereka memiliki ukuran yang sama.

Question 4: Angle \(P\) and Angle \(Q\) face the same arc. If \(P = 55^\circ\), what is \(Q\)?
Sudut \(P\) dan Sudut \(Q\) menghadap busur yang sama. Jika \(P = 55^\circ\), berapakah \(Q\)?

Question 5: If angle \(P = 2x\) and angle \(Q = 40^\circ\) face the same arc. Find \(x\).
Jika sudut \(P = 2x\) dan sudut \(Q = 40^\circ\) menghadap busur yang sama. Carilah \(x\).

Question 6: If \(P\) and \(Q\) face the same arc, and \(P\) is twice \(Q\). If \(Q = 35^\circ\), what is \(P\)?
Jika \(P\) dan \(Q\) menghadap busur yang sama, dan \(P\) dua kali \(Q\). Jika \(Q = 35^\circ\), berapakah \(P\)?

Theory: Harder Cyclic Quadrilaterals

Opposite angles add to \(180^\circ\). We can use ratios like 2:3. If angles are \(2x\) and \(3x\), then \(2x + 3x = 180^\circ\).
Sudut berhadapan dijumlah jadi \(180^\circ\). Kita bisa pakai perbandingan seperti 2:3. Jika sudutnya \(2x\) dan \(3x\), maka \(2x + 3x = 180^\circ\).

Question 7: In cyclic quadrilateral, \(\angle A = 3x+10\) and \(\angle C = 2x+20\). What is \(x\)?
Pada segi empat tali busur, \(\angle A = 3x+10\) dan \(\angle C = 2x+20\). Berapakah \(x\)?

Question 8: Using the previous question, what is the size of \(\angle A\)?
Menggunakan soal sebelumnya, berapakah ukuran \(\angle A\)?

Question 9: If \(\angle A : \angle C = 2 : 3\), what is \(\angle A\)?
Jika \(\angle A : \angle C = 2 : 3\), berapakah \(\angle A\)?

Question 10: If \(\angle B : \angle D = 1 : 2\), what is \(\angle D\)?
Jika \(\angle B : \angle D = 1 : 2\), berapakah \(\angle D\)?
