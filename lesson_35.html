Review: Exponents & Roots

Bilangan Berpangkat & Bentuk Akar

Exponents represent repeated multiplication. The square root \( (\sqrt{}) \) is the inverse operation of squaring a number. Pangkat menyatakan perkalian berulang. Akar kuadrat adalah kebalikan dari pangkat.

If \( a^2 = b \), then \( \sqrt{b} = a \). Jika \( a^2 = b \), maka \( \sqrt{b} = a \).

To The Point Formula

1. \( a^2 = a \times a \)

2. \( \sqrt{a \times a} = a \)

Rumus To The Point: Pangkat itu perkalian berulang. Akar kuadrat membalikkan proses kuadrat.

Review: The Pythagorean Theorem

Teorema Pythagoras

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle) is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides. Pada segitiga siku-siku, kuadrat sisi miring sama dengan jumlah kuadrat sisi siku-sikunya.

Hypotenuse (\( c \)) is always the longest side. Sisi miring selalu sisi terpanjang.

To The Point Formula

\( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \)

Rumus To The Point: \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \), di mana \( c \) adalah sisi miring.

Review: Pythagorean Triples

Tripel Pythagoras

Pythagorean triples are sets of three positive integers \( (a, b, c) \) that perfectly satisfy \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \). Tripel Pythagoras adalah tiga bilangan bulat positif yang memenuhi rumus Pythagoras.

Common triples: \( (3, 4, 5) \), \( (5, 12, 13) \), \( (8, 15, 17) \). Tripel yang sering muncul: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.

To The Point Formula

Memorize: \( 3^2 + 4^2 = 5^2 \) (Base for all multiples!)

Rumus To The Point: Hafalkan 3-4-5. Kelipatannya seperti 6-8-10 juga berlaku.

Real-World Connection: Ladders & Walls

Penerapan di Dunia Nyata: Tangga & Tembok

The Pythagorean theorem is highly practical! If a 5-meter ladder leans against a wall, and its base is 3 meters from the wall, we can find exactly how high the ladder reaches. Teorema Pythagoras sangat praktis! Kita bisa mencari tinggi tangga ke tembok.

Here, the ladder is the hypotenuse \( c \), the wall is \( b \), and the ground is \( a \). Tangga adalah sisi miring, tembok dan tanah adalah sisi siku-sikunya.

To The Point Formula

Identify \( c \) first (it's always opposite the right angle / usually the longest object).

Rumus To The Point: Cari dulu sisi miring (\( c \)). Sisi miring selalu di depan sudut siku-siku.

Finding Distances: Diagonals

Mencari Jarak: Diagonal

A rectangle is made of two right-angled triangles if you draw a diagonal line across it. Persegi panang dibagi menjadi dua segitiga siku-siku oleh diagonalnya.

The diagonal acts as the hypotenuse! If a TV is 80 cm wide and 60 cm tall, the diagonal screen size is \( \sqrt{80^2 + 60^2} \). Diagonal adalah sisi miring. Ukuran TV didapat dari diagonalnya.

To The Point Formula

Diagonal of Rectangle \( = \sqrt{\text{width}^2 + \text{length}^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Diagonal Persegi Panjang \( = \sqrt{p^2 + l^2} \).

Navigation & Shortest Paths

Navigasi & Jalur Tercepat

If you walk 3 km North and then 4 km East, you actually traveled 7 km. But if you could fly straight, the distance is exactly 5 km! Jika berjalan ke utara lalu ke timur, jarak tempuhnya bertambah. Tapi jarak terusannya bisa dihitung Pythagoras.

This is how GPS systems calculate straight-line distances. Begitu cara GPS menghitung jarak lurus antar dua titik.

To The Point Formula

Straight-line distance \( = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Jarak lurus tinggal akarkan jumlah kuadrat selisih koordinatnya.

Converting Roots and Exponents

Mengubah Bentuk Akar dan Pangkat

A square root \( \sqrt{x} \) asks: "What number multiplied by itself gives \( x \)?" Akar kuadrat bertanya: "Angka apa dikali dirinya sendiri menghasilkan \( x \)?"

We can write roots as fractional exponents: \( \sqrt{x} = x^{1/2} \). Kita bisa menulis akar sebagai pangkat pecahan: \( \sqrt{x} = x^{1/2} \).

To The Point Formula

\( \sqrt{x} = x^{1/2} \)    |    \( \sqrt[3]{x} = x^{1/3} \)

Rumus To The Point: Akar kuadrat sama dengan pangkat setengah.

Standard Form (Bilangan Baku)

Bilangan Baku

Standard form (scientific notation) expresses very large or small numbers easily. Bentuk baku digunakan untuk menulis angka yang sangat besar atau kecil.

Format: \( a \times 10^n \), where \( 1 \le a < 10 \) and \( n \) is an integer. Formatnya: \( a \times 10^n \), dengan \( a \) antara 1 dan 9.999...

To The Point Formula

\( a \times 10^n \) (Move decimal right = negative \( n \), left = positive \( n \))

Rumus To The Point: Geser koma ke kiri, pangkat positif. Geser ke kanan, pangkat negatif.

Isolating Variables in Pythagoras

Memisahkan Variabel di Pythagoras

Sometimes we know the hypotenuse and one side, but need to find the missing leg. Kadang kita tahu sisi miring dan satu sisi, lalu mencari sisi yang hilang.

If \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \), then \( b^2 = c^2 - a^2 \), so \( b = \sqrt{c^2 - a^2} \). Jika mencari sisi siku-siku, kurangkan kuadrat sisi miring dengan kuadrat sisi yang diketahui.

To The Point Formula

Finding a leg: \( b = \sqrt{c^2 - a^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Mencari sisi siku-siku: \( \sqrt{\text{miring}^2 - \text{sisi}^2} \).

Checking for Right Triangles

Mengecek Segitiga Siku-siku

The converse of the Pythagorean Theorem states: If \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \), then the triangle is a right-angled triangle. Kebalikan Teorema Pythagoras: Jika \( a^2 + b^2 = c^2 \), maka itu segitiga siku-siku.

This is useful to verify if a corner is perfectly 90 degrees, like in construction or carpentry. Berguna untuk memastikan sudut 90 derajat, seperti di konstruksi.

To The Point Formula

Test \( a^2 + b^2 \). If it equals \( c^2 \), it's a right triangle!

Rumus To The Point: Cek apakah jumlah kuadrat dua sisi sama dengan kuadrat sisi terpanjang.

Height of an Isosceles Triangle

Tinggi Segitiga Sama Kaki

An isosceles triangle can be split perfectly down the middle into two identical right-angled triangles. Segitiga sama kaki bisa dibagi menjadi dua segitiga siku-siku yang identik.

The base becomes half (\( a \)), the equal side becomes the hypotenuse (\( c \)), and the height is \( b \). Alas jadi setengah, sisi sama kaki jadi sisi miring, tinggi dicari dengan Pythagoras.

To The Point Formula

\( h = \sqrt{\text{side}^2 - (\frac{\text{base}}{2})^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Bagi alas dua, lalu cari sisi siku-sikunya.

Diagonals in 3D: The Box Problem

Diagonal di Ruang 3D: Balok

To find the longest diagonal inside a rectangular box (room), we use the 3D Pythagorean theorem. Untuk mencari diagonal ruang di balok, gunakan Pythagoras 3D.

First find the base diagonal, then use that with the height to find the space diagonal. Cari diagonal alas dulu, lalu gabungkan dengan tinggi.

To The Point Formula

Space Diagonal \( = \sqrt{l^2 + w^2 + h^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Diagonal ruang \( = \sqrt{p^2 + l^2 + t^2} \).

Step-by-Step: Finding Hypotenuse

Langkah demi Langkah: Mencari Sisi Miring

Let's do it together! A triangle has legs \( a = 6 \) cm and \( b = 8 \) cm. Mari coba bersama! Segitiga dengan sisi 6 cm dan 8 cm.

Step 1: \( 6^2 = 36 \), \( 8^2 = 64 \).
Step 2: \( 36 + 64 = 100 \).
Step 3: \( c = \sqrt{100} = 10 \) cm. Langkah 1: Kuadratkan. Langkah 2: Jumlahkan. Langkah 3: Akarkan.

To The Point Formula

\( c = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2} \) -> Square, Add, Root!

Rumus To The Point: Kuadratkan, jumlahkan, lalu akarkan!

Step-by-Step: Finding a Leg

Langkah demi Langkah: Mencari Sisi Siku-siku

What if we know the hypotenuse (\( c \)) and one leg (\( b \)), but need to find the other leg (\( a \))? Bagaimana jika kita tahu sisi miring dan satu sisi siku-siku?

Subtract the square of the leg from the square of the hypotenuse, then take the square root. Kurangkan kuadrat sisi miring dengan kuadrat sisi yang diketahui, lalu akarkan.

To The Point Formula

\( a = \sqrt{c^2 - b^2} \) -> Square, Subtract, Root!

Rumus To The Point: Kuadratkan, kurangkan, lalu akarkan!

Spotting Pythagorean Triples Fast

Mengenali Tripel Pythagoras dengan Cepat

If you spot numbers that are multiples of basic triples, you don't need to calculate! Jika angkanya kelipatan tripel dasar, tidak perlu menghitung manual!

If legs are 18 and 24, notice they are \( 6 \times 3 \) and \( 6 \times 4 \). So the hypotenuse is \( 6 \times 5 = 30 \). Jika sisi 18 dan 24, itu kelipatan 6 dari tripel 3-4-5. Sisi miringnya 30.

To The Point Formula

Scale factor: If \( a,b,c \) is a triple, so is \( ka, kb, kc \).

Rumus To The Point: Kelipatan tripel juga merupakan tripel.

Distance on a Coordinate Grid

Jarak pada Koordinat Kartesius

To find the distance between two points \( (x_1, y_1) \) and \( (x_2, y_2) \), we treat the horizontal and vertical distances as the legs of a right triangle. Jarak antar dua titik bisa dicari dengan memperlakukan selisih x dan y sebagai sisi siku-siku.

Distance \( = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \). Rumus jarak dua titik.

To The Point Formula

\( d = \sqrt{(\Delta x)^2 + (\Delta y)^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Jarak = akar dari kuadrat selisih x ditambah kuadrat selisih y.

Classifying Triangles by Angles

Mengklasifikasikan Segitiga berdasarkan Sudut

Using the Pythagorean inequalities, we can determine if a triangle is acute, right, or obtuse without using a protractor. Dengan Pythagoras, kita bisa tahu jenis segitiga tanpa busur derajat.

Assume \( c \) is the longest side.
If \( c^2 < a^2 + b^2 \), it's acute.
If \( c^2 = a^2 + b^2 \), it's right.
If \( c^2 > a^2 + b^2 \), it's obtuse. Asumsikan \( c \) sisi terpanjang. Bandingkan kuadratnya.

To The Point Formula

Compare \( c^2 \) with \( a^2 + b^2 \): Less than, Equal to, or Greater than.

Rumus To The Point: Bandingkan kuadrat sisi miring dengan jumlah kuadrat sisi lain.

The Sliding Ladder Problem

Masalah Tangga Meluncur

A ladder of fixed length leans against a wall. If the base slides away from the wall, the top must slide down. Tangga dengan panjang tetap bersandar di tembok. Jika dasarnya menjauh, atasnya turun.

The ladder length (\( c \)) remains constant! Use the new base (\( a \)) to find the new height (\( b \)). Panjang tangga tetap! Gunakan alas baru untuk mencari tinggi baru.

To The Point Formula

Constant \( c \). Find new \( b = \sqrt{c^2 - a_{new}^2} \)

Rumus To The Point: Sisi miring tetap. Cari tinggi baru dengan rumus mencari sisi siku-siku.
