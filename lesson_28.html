Spiral Review Warm-up

Before we learn about circles, we need to sharpen our algebra skills. Algebra uses letters to represent unknown numbers.

Sebelum kita belajar tentang lingkaran, kita harus menajamkan kemampuan aljabar kita. Aljabar menggunakan huruf untuk mewakili angka yang tidak diketahui.

  • Variable: A letter representing an unknown number. Variabel: Huruf yang mewakili angka tidak diketahui.
  • Constant: A fixed number. Konstanta: Angka tetap.
  • Coefficient: The number multiplied by the variable. Koefisien: Angka dikalikan dengan variabel.

1. Identify the variable in the expression: \(5x + 12\)

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The variable is 'x'.
Variabelnya adalah 'x'.

2. What is the coefficient of \(y\) in the expression \(8 - 3y\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The coefficient is the number directly in front of the variable \(y\). Here, it is \(-3\).
Koefisien adalah angka tepat di depan variabel \(y\). Di sini, angkanya \(-3\).

3. Identify the constant in the expression: \(5x + 12\)

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The constant is the number without a variable, which is 12.
Konstanta adalah angka tanpa variabel, yaitu 12.

We can translate sentences into algebraic expressions.

Kita dapat menerjemahkan kalimat menjadi ekspresi aljabar.

Example: "Seven more than three times a number (n)".

Tiga kali nomor \(n\) adalah \(3n\). Tujuh lebihnya berarti kita tambah 7: \(3n + 7\).

Dua kali \(y\) adalah \(2y\). Dikurangi 4 berarti kurangi 4: \(2y - 4\).

Introduction & Real-World Connection

Time: 00:10 - 00:15

Imagine you are a NASA engineer designing a satellite path. The path must only "touch" the Earth's atmosphere at exactly ONE point before flying into space. This line is called a Tangent.

Bayangkan kamu adalah insinyur NASA yang merancang jalur satelit. Jalur tersebut harus "menyentuh" atmosfer Bumi tepat di SATU titik sebelum terbang ke luar angkasa. Garis ini disebut Garis Singgung.

1. How many points does a tangent line touch on a circle?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
A tangent touches exactly one point.
Garis singgung menyentuh tepat satu titik.

2. Look at a bicycle chain. The straight part of the chain is a tangent to the gear (circle). True or False?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
True! It touches the gear at one point.
Benar! Rantai menyentuh gir di satu titik.

3. A line that cuts through a circle at two points is called a Secant, not a tangent.

Explanation / Penjelasan:
True. A secant crosses the circle twice.
Benar. Garis sekan memotong lingkaran dua kali.

Let's visualize it. If you have a circle and you draw a line that only touches the edge, that's the tangent. The point where it touches is the Point of Tangency.

Mari kita visualisasikan. Jika kamu memiliki lingkaran dan menggambar garis yang hanya menyentuh tepinya, itu adalah garis singgung. Titik di mana ia menyentuh adalah Titik Singgung.

1. What do we call the point where the tangent touches the circle?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Point of Tangency.
Titik Singgung.

2. Can a tangent line pass through the inside of a circle?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
No. If it went inside, it would intersect at two points.
Tidak. Jika masuk ke dalam, ia akan memotong di dua titik.

3. Which of these is an example of a tangent in real life?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
A road touching a roundabout touches it at one point.
Jalan yang menyentuh bundaran menyentuhnya di satu titik.

The most important property of a tangent is the direction it touches the circle. It doesn't just touch it randomly; it touches it precisely at a 90-degree angle (perpendicular) to the radius. We will prove this in the next section.

Sifat terpenting dari garis singgung adalah arah saat ia menyentuh lingkaran. Ia tidak menyentuhnya secara acak; ia menyentuhnya tepat pada sudut 90 derajat (tegak lurus) terhadap jari-jari. Kita akan membuktikan ini di bagian selanjutnya.

1. What angle should a tangent make with the radius at the point of tangency?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
90 degrees (perpendicular).
90 derajat (tegak lurus).

2. The line from the center of the circle to the point of tangency is called the...

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Radius.
Jari-jari.

3. If the radius is perpendicular to a line at a point on the circle, that line must be a...

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Tangent.
Garis Singgung.

4. Tangent comes from the Latin word "tangere". What does it mean?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
"Tangere" means "to touch".
"Tangere" berarti "menyentuh".

Review of Prior Knowledge

Time: 00:15 - 00:20

Remember the Pythagorean Theorem! For a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse (the longest side, \(c\)) is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides (\(a\) and \(b\)).

Ingat Teorema Pythagoras! Untuk segitiga siku-siku, kuadrat dari hipotenusa (sisi terpanjang, \(c\)) sama dengan jumlah kuadrat dari dua sisi lainnya (\(a\) dan \(b\)).

\(a^2 + b^2 = c^2\)

1. If \(a = 9\) and \(b = 12\), what is the length of \(c\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225\). \(\sqrt{225} = 15\).
\(9^2 + 12^2 = 81 + 144 = 225\). \(\sqrt{225} = 15\).

2. What do we call the longest side of a right-angled triangle?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The longest side is called the Hypotenuse.
Sisi terpanjang disebut Hipotenusa.

3. If \(c = 15\) and \(a = 9\), find \(b\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(b^2 = c^2 - a^2 = 225 - 81 = 144\). \(\sqrt{144} = 12\).
\(b^2 = c^2 - a^2 = 225 - 81 = 144\). \(\sqrt{144} = 12\).

Perpendicular lines intersect at exactly 90 degrees. The key rule for tangents: A tangent line is ALWAYS perpendicular (90 degrees) to the radius of the circle at the point of tangency.

Garis tegak lurus berpotongan tepat di 90 derajat. Aturan kunci untuk garis singgung: Garis singgung SELALU tegak lurus (90 derajat) dengan jari-jari lingkaran pada titik singgungnya.

1. What angle does a tangent make with the radius?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
90 degrees (perpendicular).
90 derajat (tegak lurus).

2. If the radius is 5 cm and a line touches the circle at exactly one point, what is the angle between them?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
90 degrees, because the line is a tangent.
90 derajat, karena garis tersebut adalah garis singgung.

3. Can a tangent ever make a 45-degree angle with the radius?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
No. It must always be 90 degrees.
Tidak. Harus selalu 90 derajat.

When we draw a tangent from an external point \(P\) to a circle with center \(O\), it touches the circle at point \(A\). This creates Triangle \(OAP\), which is a right-angled triangle at \(A\)! The radius \(OA\) is one leg, the tangent \(PA\) is another leg, and the distance \(OP\) is the hypotenuse.

Ketika kita menggambar garis singgung dari titik luar \(P\) ke lingkaran dengan pusat \(O\), ia menyentuh lingkaran di titik \(A\). Ini membentuk Segitiga \(OAP\), yang merupakan segitiga siku-siku di \(A\)! Jari-jari \(OA\) adalah satu sisi, garis singgung \(PA\) adalah sisi lainnya, dan jarak \(OP\) adalah hipotenusa.

1. In Triangle \(OAP\), where is the right angle located?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The right angle is at the point of tangency, point A.
Sudut siku-siku berada di titik singgung, yaitu titik A.

2. In Triangle \(OAP\), which side is the hypotenuse?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OP\) is the hypotenuse because it is opposite the 90-degree angle at \(A\).
\(OP\) adalah hipotenusa karena berhadapan dengan sudut 90 derajat di \(A\).

3. If \(OA = 6\) cm and \(PA = 8\) cm, what is \(OP\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OP^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100\). \(\sqrt{100} = 10\).
\(OP^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100\). \(\sqrt{100} = 10\).

4. Why is \(OA\) perpendicular to \(PA\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
A tangent is always perpendicular to the radius.
Garis singgung selalu tegak lurus dengan jari-jari.

Problem Introduction

Time: 00:20 - 00:30

Let's calculate the length of a tangent! We have point \(P\) outside the circle. The distance from center \(O\) to \(P\) is 13 cm. The radius \(OA\) is 5 cm. We need to find the tangent length \(PA\).

Mari kita hitung panjang garis singgung! Kita memiliki titik \(P\) di luar lingkaran. Jarak dari pusat \(O\) ke \(P\) adalah 13 cm. Jari-jari \(OA\) adalah 5 cm. Kita perlu mencari panjang garis singgung \(PA\).

1. In Triangle \(OAP\), what is the formula for \(PA\) using Pythagoras?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Since \(OP\) is the hypotenuse, \(PA^2 = OP^2 - OA^2\).
Karena \(OP\) adalah hipotenusa, maka \(PA^2 = OP^2 - OA^2\).

2. Calculate \(PA\) if \(OP = 13\) and \(OA = 5\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(PA^2 = 13^2 - 5^2 = 169 - 25 = 144\). \(\sqrt{144} = 12\).
\(PA^2 = 13^2 - 5^2 = 169 - 25 = 144\). \(\sqrt{144} = 12\).

3. If \(OP = 10\) and \(OA = 8\), find \(PA\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(PA^2 = 10^2 - 8^2 = 100 - 64 = 36\). \(\sqrt{36} = 6\).
\(PA^2 = 10^2 - 8^2 = 100 - 64 = 36\). \(\sqrt{36} = 6\).

Let's do it step-by-step with algebra! Substitute the values into the formula: \(PA^2 = 13^2 - 5^2\). Calculate the squares: \(PA^2 = 169 - 25\). Subtract: \(PA^2 = 144\). Find the square root: \(PA = 12\) cm.

Mari kita lakukan langkah demi langkah dengan aljabar! Substitusikan nilai ke dalam rumus: \(PA^2 = 13^2 - 5^2\). Hitung kuadratnya: \(PA^2 = 169 - 25\). Kurangkan: \(PA^2 = 144\). Cari akar kuadratnya: \(PA = 12\) cm.

1. What is \(15^2\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(15 \times 15 = 225\).
\(15 \times 15 = 225\).

2. What is \(\sqrt{144}\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(12 \times 12 = 144\).
\(12 \times 12 = 144\).

3. If \(PA^2 = 400\), what is \(PA\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(\sqrt{400} = 20\).
\(\sqrt{400} = 20\).

What if we know the tangent and radius, and want to find the distance to the center? We just change the formula! \(OP^2 = OA^2 + PA^2\). This means we add the squares instead of subtracting.

Bagaimana jika kita tahu garis singgung dan jari-jari, dan ingin mencari jarak ke pusat? Kita cukup mengubah rumusnya! \(OP^2 = OA^2 + PA^2\). Ini berarti kita menjumlahkan kuadratnya alih-alih menguranginya.

1. If \(PA = 8\) and \(OA = 6\), find \(OP\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OP^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100\). \(\sqrt{100} = 10\).
\(OP^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 36 + 64 = 100\). \(\sqrt{100} = 10\).

2. If \(PA = 12\) and \(OP = 13\), find \(OA\) (the radius).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OA^2 = 13^2 - 12^2 = 169 - 144 = 25\). \(\sqrt{25} = 5\).
\(OA^2 = 13^2 - 12^2 = 169 - 144 = 25\). \(\sqrt{25} = 5\).

3. If \(OA = 8\) and \(PA = 15\), find \(OP\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OP^2 = 8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = 289\). \(\sqrt{289} = 17\).
\(OP^2 = 8^2 + 15^2 = 64 + 225 = 289\). \(\sqrt{289} = 17\).

4. Which side in Triangle \(OAP\) is always the hypotenuse?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(OP\), the line from the center to the external point, is always the longest side.
\(OP\), garis dari pusat ke titik luar, selalu menjadi sisi terpanjang.

Guided Practice

Time: 00:30 - 00:45

Let's explore a special property! If we draw TWO tangents from the SAME external point \(P\) to the circle, touching at points \(A\) and \(B\), the lengths are ALWAYS equal. \(PA = PB\).

Mari kita jelajahi sifat spesial! Jika kita menarik DUA garis singgung dari titik luar yang SAMA \(P\) ke lingkaran, menyentuh di titik \(A\) dan \(B\), panjangnya SELALU sama. \(PA = PB\).

1. If \(PA = 10\) cm, what is the length of \(PB\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Because they are tangents from the same external point, \(PA = PB = 10\) cm.
Karena merupakan garis singgung dari titik luar yang sama, \(PA = PB = 10\) cm.

2. The shape formed by connecting \(O, A, P, B\) is called a...

Explanation / Penjelasan:
It is a Kite (Layang-layang) because \(OA = OB\) (radii) and \(PA = PB\) (tangents).
Ini adalah Layang-layang karena \(OA = OB\) (jari-jari) dan \(PA = PB\) (garis singgung).

3. If \(PA = 2x + 5\) and \(PB = 15\), what is the first algebraic equation you should write?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Since \(PA = PB\), set them equal: \(2x + 5 = 15\).
Karena \(PA = PB\), samakan keduanya: \(2x + 5 = 15\).

Let's solve the equation \(2x + 5 = 15\). Subtract 5 from both sides: \(2x = 10\). Divide by 2: \(x = 5\). The value of \(x\) is 5! And the length of the tangent is 15.

Mari kita selesaikan persamaan \(2x + 5 = 15\). Kurangi 5 dari kedua sisi: \(2x = 10\). Bagi dengan 2: \(x = 5\). Nilai \(x\) adalah 5! Dan panjang garis singgungnya adalah 15.

1. Solve for \(x\): \(3x - 4 = 11\)

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(3x = 11 + 4 \rightarrow 3x = 15 \rightarrow x = 5\).
\(3x = 11 + 4 \rightarrow 3x = 15 \rightarrow x = 5\).

2. If \(PA = x + 7\) and \(PB = 22\), find \(x\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
\(x + 7 = 22 \rightarrow x = 22 - 7 = 15\).
\(x + 7 = 22 \rightarrow x = 22 - 7 = 15\).

3. If \(PA = 4x\) and \(PB = 20\), find the length of \(PA\).

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Since \(PA = PB\), \(PA\) must be 20 (and \(x=5\)).
Karena \(PA = PB\), \(PA\) pasti 20 (dan \(x=5\)).

Look at the Kite \(OAPB\). It consists of two identical right-angled triangles: \(\triangle OAP\) and \(\triangle OBP\). The angles at \(A\) and \(B\) are both 90 degrees. The line \(OP\) splits the kite perfectly in half!

Lihat Layang-layang \(OAPB\). Ini terdiri dari dua segitiga siku-siku yang identik: \(\triangle OAP\) dan \(\triangle OBP\). Sudut di \(A\) dan \(B\) keduanya 90 derajat. Garis \(OP\) membelah layang-layang menjadi dua bagian sama persis!

1. In Kite \(OAPB\), if angle \(OAP\) is 90°, what is angle \(OBP\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Both are points of tangency, so both are 90°.
Keduanya adalah titik singgung, jadi keduanya 90°.

2. Which line divides the Kite \(OAPB\) into two identical right triangles?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
The line from the center to the external point (\(OP\)) divides the kite.
Garis dari pusat ke titik luar (\(OP\)) membelah layang-layang.

3. If \(OA = 5\) and \(PA = 12\), what is the perimeter of Kite \(OAPB\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
Perimeter = \(OA + OB + PA + PB = 5 + 5 + 12 + 12 = 34\).
Keliling = \(OA + OB + PA + PB = 5 + 5 + 12 + 12 = 34\).

4. In \(\triangle OAP\), angle \(OAP = 90^\circ\). What is the relationship between \(\triangle OAP\) and \(\triangle OBP\)?

Explanation / Penjelasan:
They are identical (congruent) because \(OA=OB\), \(PA=PB\), and \(OP\) is shared.
Mereka identik (kongruen) karena \(OA=OB\), \(PA=PB\), dan \(OP\) dimiliki bersama.

Key Takeaways Poin Kunci

  • 90 Degrees: A tangent is always perpendicular to the radius at the point of tangency. 90 Derajat: Garis singgung selalu tegak lurus dengan jari-jari pada titik singgung.
